# goattech launcher roadmap

what's being worked on, in plain words. no dates: things are now, next or later. this page is only about the launcher - the engine's own roadmap is upstream's and lives at [docs/engine/ROADMAP.md](docs/engine/ROADMAP.md).

<!-- [flip at release] this draft is written for the day v1 ships. "now" is post-v1 work. -->

## now

- **keep v1 healthy.** real-player bug reports, crash fixes, small polish. v1 is the start, not the finish.
- **client-specific settings sync.** standalone clients get the same shared-or-separate choice as ordinary minecraft settings. cut from v1 to keep the release tight; it is the first thing after.

## next

- **mac.** the build exists, the testing has not. sign-in, launch and play on real mac hardware.
- **verified standalone client installs.** lunar-style installs done properly, one at a time, only when verified.
- **forge and neoforge played end to end.** their installers already run; a full signed-in game launch is the remaining step.

## later

- modpacks, servers and shared worlds as first-class citizens of the launcher.
- keeping pace with upstream horizon as it follows each minecraft version.
- speed, customization and polish, always. the bar that v1 passed - an independent review above 9 out of 10 of the actual running app - holds for every version after it.
